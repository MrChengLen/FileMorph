### Security — pypdf 6.21.4

pypdf moves from 6.21.2 to 6.21.4, which fixes the seven advisories published
on 2026-10-30, all rated high: long runtimes and large memory use on crafted
PDFs (GHSA-42gh-23hr-vphw, GHSA-4vqv-7265-gm2w, GHSA-5849-834g-hvw5,
GHSA-62m5-6wh8-28x7, GHSA-cggf-277g-3q54, GHSA-mfr5-77xh-9j7h,
GHSA-x3rh-gjgm-fmqc). The floor in `requirements.txt` moves to `pypdf>=6.21.4`,
so a later re-lock cannot fall back below it.
