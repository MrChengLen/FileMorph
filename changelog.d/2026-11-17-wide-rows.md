### Changed — very wide uncompressed images are read faster

An uncompressed TIFF or BMP with very wide rows is read faster: Pillow now
reads its pixels a row or so at a time instead of in small pieces, on every
page of a TIFF. A TIFF whose tiles are far wider than its page, and an icon
(ICO) whose BMP picture is wider than an icon can be (256 pixels), are
refused as damaged, like other unreadable images (`400`, `invalid_input`).
