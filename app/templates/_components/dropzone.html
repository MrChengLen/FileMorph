The body (the hidden , the idle and the selected state) is yielded via {{ caller() }}. label — aria-label of the field (it acts as one button). describedby — id of the visible step label of a second field on a page. compact — less padding, for a second field inside a step. The default is 16 px on a phone and 24 px from 640 px, so the field stays in the first screen of a 1280 × 600 laptop window. attrs — extra data-* attributes as a dict, e.g. the convert tool's data-preset-*.
